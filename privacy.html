InspectEquip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 — Version 1.7

InspectEquip™ ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a software application operated by Vortech Mechanical Services Pty Ltd (ACN 655 628 050), an Australian registered company.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the InspectEquip mobile application.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "reports" include inspection reports, condition reports (such as pre-start, pre-hire, return and handover inspections), service reports, breakdown reports, repair reports, and other reporting workflows that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

1. Information We Handle

InspectEquip is designed primarily as an offline-first application. Information entered into the App is generally stored locally on your device and is used to provide the App's core functionality.

Depending on how you use the App, information handled by the App may include:

  • company and business details;
  • customer information entered by you;
  • vehicle, machinery, equipment and asset details;
  • report notes, checklist responses, photos, signatures, records and reports;
  • other user-generated report content;
  • licence keys, subscription identifiers and subscription status information;
  • device identifiers and device details used for subscription management and free-report access;
  • recovery email addresses and verification information, where provided;
  • limited free-report usage and app activity information; and
  • technical diagnostic and error-reporting information.

Device information may include the device model, operating system, app version, platform, country or region derived from device settings, and timezone.

We do not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is reasonably required to operate, secure, support and improve the App.

2. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App is used to:

  • create and manage reports;
  • generate PDF reports and related documents;
  • provide core application functionality;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve the reliability of the App;
  • collect limited technical diagnostic data;
  • support subscription, licence verification, device registration and recovery features;
  • manage free-report allowances and prevent repeated claims of free access;
  • understand free-report usage and conversion activity; and
  • respond to support, help and feedback requests submitted by you.

We do not sell, rent or trade user data.

3. Subscription, Device Identification and Recovery Information

InspectEquip currently uses a licence-based system rather than mandatory user accounts.

If you activate a subscription or licence, the following information may be stored and processed:

  • licence key and subscription identifier;
  • device identifiers associated with the subscription;
  • device details such as device name, model and platform;
  • subscription status and registered-device information;
  • recovery email address, if provided; and
  • recovery email verification status and related verification request data.

InspectEquip uses randomly generated identifiers stored on the device to support device registration and licence management.

The App may also use platform-provided device identifiers and derived identifier hashes for free-access management and prevention of repeated free-report claims.

On supported Apple devices, this may include Apple's Identifier for Vendor. On supported Android devices, this may include a platform-provided Android identifier.

These identifiers are used for application access management, subscription administration and free-report enforcement. They are not used for advertising or tracking activity across unrelated companies' apps or websites.

Recovery email addresses are used for:

  • verifying ownership of a subscription;
  • enabling subscription recovery and management of registered devices;
  • authorising sensitive actions such as transferring or recovering access; and
  • important communications relating to subscription access, billing, verification and recovery.

Recovery email addresses are not used for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Free Reports and Usage Tracking

InspectEquip may provide a limited number of free reports before a paid subscription is required.

To manage this allowance, the App may collect and store:

  • randomly generated installation or free-access identifiers;
  • hashed platform-provided identifiers;
  • the number of free reports used and remaining;
  • report generation events and report types;
  • limited device and app version information; and
  • information used to determine whether free access has previously been claimed.

These records help enforce the free-report allowance and prevent repeated claims following reinstallation or other changes to an app installation.

While users are accessing the free-report allowance, InspectEquip also collects limited app activity and conversion information.

This may include:

  • selected page views and navigation events;
  • starting inspections, services, repairs and breakdown reports;
  • progressing through reporting workflows;
  • saving drafts, cancelling reports and completing reports; and
  • free-report usage and subscription conversion events.

This information helps us understand how free access is used, identify difficulties in the reporting process and improve the App.

Once a user converts to a paid subscription, free-access conversion tracking stops.

Subscription verification, registered-device management, essential service records and technical crash diagnostics may continue as necessary to provide and maintain the App.

We do not use free-report activity information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

5. Data Storage

Report data is primarily stored locally on your device and/or within storage services selected, configured or controlled by you.

Photographs captured or selected within the App, including photographs added or modified when editing completed reports, are generally stored within the App's local storage.

Editing a completed report may replace previously stored report information, photographs and generated PDF files. Earlier versions may not be retained or recoverable.

Users are responsible for managing and retaining copies of report data and photographs where required for business, legal or record-keeping purposes.

Vortech Mechanical Services Pty Ltd does not routinely store customer, vehicle or report content on company-operated servers.

InspectEquip may allow users to export and import customer, vehicle and report data for backup or device transfer purposes. Exported files are created locally and are not automatically uploaded to or stored by Vortech Mechanical Services Pty Ltd.

Limited subscription, device registration, recovery, free-report usage, conversion activity and support-related information may be stored using Firebase and other third-party infrastructure services.

You are responsible for:

  • managing backups;
  • maintaining appropriate data retention practices;
  • securing your device and any storage locations used by you;
  • securing exported backup or transfer files; and
  • complying with privacy, record-keeping and legal obligations applicable to your business.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not routinely receive or share customer, vehicle or report content stored locally within the App.

Information may be processed or disclosed:

  • where required by law or applicable legal obligations;
  • where necessary to provide subscription, billing, verification or recovery services;
  • where required for app distribution, infrastructure, diagnostics or technical support;
  • through service providers supporting the App's operation; or
  • where you choose to export, transmit or share information yourself.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

7. App Stores and Third-Party Services

If you purchase or manage a subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, billing and subscription-related information may be collected and processed by those platform providers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

InspectEquip uses third-party services to support its functionality.

These may include:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud services for licensing, free-report management, app activity records, cloud functions and infrastructure;
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics and application stability;
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution and subscription services; and
  • third-party email delivery services for verification emails and support communications.

These providers may process limited technical, subscription, device or communication information as required to deliver their services.

Their handling of information is also subject to their applicable privacy policies and service terms.

8. Diagnostics and Error Reporting

InspectEquip may collect limited technical diagnostic information to identify errors, improve performance and maintain application reliability.

This may include:

  • crash reports and error logs;
  • device type, operating system version and app version;
  • technical information relating to application performance and failures; and
  • limited device or subscription-related context necessary to troubleshoot problems.

Diagnostic data does not intentionally include report content, photos, customer details or operational information unless such information is manually provided in a support or feedback request or inadvertently appears in diagnostic information.

Where reasonably possible, diagnostic information is minimised and used for troubleshooting, maintenance and improvement of the App.

Crash diagnostics may continue for subscribed users after free-access conversion tracking has stopped.

Diagnostic information is not used for third-party advertising.

9. Feedback and Support Requests

If you contact us through the in-app feedback form, we may collect and process:

  • your name and email address;
  • the message or request you submit;
  • optional subscription and registered-device details, if provided; and
  • technical context relevant to your request.

This information is used to respond to enquiries, provide support and improve the App.

Feedback submissions may be transmitted and processed through third-party email delivery providers for message delivery and support handling.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed within the App.

InspectEquip may provide optional security features, including PIN access control, to help restrict unauthorised access.

Certain locally stored information and identifiers may be protected using secure storage or encryption mechanisms.

Security also depends on your mobile device, operating system, user permissions and any storage solutions you use.

You are responsible for securing your device, enabling device-level protection where available and controlling physical access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. International Use

InspectEquip may be used internationally.

Limited information processed through Firebase, app store services, diagnostic providers or email delivery services may be processed or stored in jurisdictions outside your country of residence, depending on the provider and service configuration.

Information entered into reports may also be stored in locations selected or controlled by you.

12. Future Features

We may introduce additional report types, workflows, cloud synchronisation, analytics, backups, integrations, account functionality or other online services in future versions of the App.

If introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain how those features collect, use, store and protect information.

13. Children's Privacy

InspectEquip is a professional business tool and is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age.

14. Access, Correction and Data Deletion Requests

You may contact us at support@inspectequip.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by us, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

This may include:

  • recovery email addresses;
  • support or feedback request information;
  • subscription or device registration records;
  • free-access identifiers and associated usage records;
  • app activity or conversion records associated with identifiable records; and
  • other limited personal information associated with app support or subscription management.

We will take reasonable steps to process legitimate deletion requests in accordance with applicable legal, operational, fraud-prevention and record-keeping obligations.

Certain subscription or transaction records may need to be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary to maintain subscription entitlements and prevent misuse.

Deleting records associated with a subscription or free-report allowance may affect our ability to verify or provide continued access.

Report data, including photos, signatures and operational information stored locally on your device or in storage systems controlled by you, remains your responsibility to manage and delete.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes will be published within the App and on our website.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes in how personal information is processed.

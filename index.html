How InspectEquip Works

Start a Report

Choose an inspection, service, breakdown or repair report and enter the customer and vehicle details.

Capture, Mark Up & Explain

Add photos, highlight defects with arrows, circles and drawings, and include comments or technician notes.

Generate a Professional PDF

Create a customer-ready report that can be saved, shared or sent in minutes.

Highlight Defects Directly on Photos

Use InspectEquip's image annotation tools to point out cracks, leaks, wear, damage and other areas needing attention before the photo is added to the finished report.

Make every finding easier to understand

  • Add arrows, circles or ovals.
  • Choose a visible annotation colour for each photo.
  • Resize, rotate and reposition annotations.
  • Include the marked-up image in the customer's PDF report.

Instead of just attaching a photo, clearly show the customer exactly where the problem is.
